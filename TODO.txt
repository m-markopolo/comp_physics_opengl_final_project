Final Exercises:

    Make a scenery with Didymos and Dimorphos asteroids.
      Dimorphos is orbiting around Didymos in a circular orbit at 1.19 kilometers 
      with a period of 12 hours.
      Didymos model is already in kilometers and Dimoprhos should be realised 
      as an ellipsoid with semiaxis 0.4, 0.3, 0.2.
      Didymos spins with period 2.3 hours and Dimorphos is tidally locked.
      Dimorphos is tidally locked, which mean its rotational period is the same with its orbital period.
      Make a programme that simulates the system with directional light.
      Use didymos2019final.obj model but you need to compute the normals in some way. (4 points)

    Make a scenery with 4 items of your choice. Use positional light and allow the user to move the light source and light color with a control of your choice (either mouse or keyboard). (2 points)

    Free project. Be inspiring to the future students of the class. (4 points)
